This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use GSG.

We may collect certain information when you use the Game, including:

Microphone

The Game may access and record audio from your microphone when microphone features are used. Microphone audio is not stored by us.

We may use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To protect our rights, property, and safety, as well as those of our users

We may also use personal information for marketing purposes where permitted by applicable law. You may opt out of certain marketing communications by following the instructions provided in those communications.

3. Sharing Your Information

We may share your information with third parties when reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including:

  • Service Providers: Companies or individuals that assist us in operating, maintaining, supporting, or improving the Game.
  • Legal Requirements: Third parties when we are required to disclose information in response to lawful requests, court orders, legal processes, or applicable laws.
  • Business Transfers: Information may be transferred in connection with a merger, sale of assets, acquisition, reorganization, or other transaction involving all or part of our business.
  • Protection of Rights and Safety: Where reasonably necessary to protect our rights, property, safety, users, or others.

4. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Access your personal information and receive a copy of it

Please note that choosing not to provide certain information may limit your ability to use some features of the Game. To exercise any applicable rights, please contact us using the information provided in the Contact Us section below.

You may request that we delete personal information that we have collected or stored about you. To make a deletion request, please contact us at d4rkytreal@gmail.com.

We will review and respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe and take reasonable steps to delete eligible personal information from our systems, subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate business needs.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your personal information.

7. Children's Privacy

The Game is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we may post the updated version on our website or otherwise make it available through the Game. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your personal information.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at d4rkytreal@gmail.com.

We are committed to addressing privacy-related questions and concerns as reasonably and promptly as possible.
